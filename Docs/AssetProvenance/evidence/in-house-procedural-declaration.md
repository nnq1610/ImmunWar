# In-house procedural asset declaration

Date: 2026-09-18 UTC

The vessel tiles, defense-node graphic, simple VFX textures, ATP icon, and two synthesized P0 sound effects were generated specifically for Immune War by `Assets/ImmunWar/Scripts/Editor/P0ContentGenerator.cs`. The generator uses only mathematical shapes, colors, and synthesized waveforms; it does not embed third-party source media.

The project may use, modify, and redistribute these outputs with the game. Human gameplay-scale visual/audio review is still required before changing their asset records from `Review` to `Approved`.
