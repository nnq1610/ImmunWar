# Medical and educational content review

Date: 2026-09-18

- Immune War is explicitly described as a fictional strategy game, not medical advice.
- Unit and pathogen names are used as broad educational metaphors; stats, combat, Fever Mode, mutation, infection, and organ routes are fictional mechanics and must not be presented as diagnostic or therapeutic facts.
- Copy avoids claims that a specific immune cell, fever response, or treatment always causes a real-world outcome.
- Infection, mutation, and fever states use neutral language without instructions for self-treatment.
- English and Vietnamese first-battle guidance includes the non-medical disclaimer.

Approved wording is stored in `Assets/ImmunWar/Data/Localization/en-US.json` and `vi-VN.json`. Future copy changes involving health outcomes require another content review.
