# Asset Review Recommendations

Review date: 2026-09-18

## Recommended visual direction

Keep the glossy, cute biological character style established by the four P0 generated units. It provides the clearest faction language: defenders use cyan/blue/yellow with open friendly expressions; enemies use red, magenta, toxic green, asymmetry, and sharper expressions.

## Best current candidates

1. `DEF001` Macrophage — keep; distinctive engulfing silhouette and strong tank/readability cues.
2. `DEF002` T-Cell — keep; attack pose and energy projectile clearly communicate damage role.
3. `DEF006` Energy Cell — keep; yellow palette, mitochondria and lightning motif communicate ATP generation.
4. `ENE001` Virus — keep; hostile red palette and spiked circular silhouette establish the enemy visual language.
5. `DEF003` B-Cell v2 — recommended over the geometric placeholder.
6. `DEF004` NK Cell v2 — recommended over the geometric placeholder.
7. `ENE002` Bacteria v2 — recommended over the geometric placeholder.
8. `ENE003` Mutant Virus v2 — recommended over the geometric placeholder.
9. `DEF005` Platelet v3 — recommended for a separate approval pass; golden-peach/amber and teal now distinguish it from hostile red enemies.

## Replace before approval

- `ENV001`–`ENV004`: current vessel and node images are functional geometric placeholders, not production environment art.
- `VFX001`, `VFX003`, `VFX007`: current flat circles/blocks do not match the rendering quality of the characters.
- `UI007` and most tactical/final icons: current flat shapes are adequate for tests only.
- `DEF005` runtime file: keep as a placeholder until the v3 candidate receives separate approval and is exported.
- Organ backgrounds, boss art, and remaining final presentation assets require a separate visual pass.

## Approval order

1. Approve or reject the four P0 character candidates.
2. Approve/reject the five Tactical Units v2 candidates.
3. Produce a coherent lung environment kit and defense node.
4. Produce gameplay-scale VFX and UI icons derived from the selected character palette.
5. Review audio in the representative game mix.
6. Complete organ, boss, and final presentation review, then rerun the 51-item release gate.

No item in this document is automatically approved. A recommendation records visual suitability only; it does not replace human rights, medical-content, or audio review.
